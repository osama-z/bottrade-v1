Follow the journey of a single market tick. Watch how raw exchange data is transformed into a highly-calculated, risk-managed trade executed automatically.

The bot listens to Binance. When a 1-hour candle closes, it pulls the raw price history and cleans out any API errors or missing gaps.

⚖️ The Combiner

The "Smart Judge" fuses the 3 AI brains together using the 40/40/20 rule. It checks if the overall confidence is high enough to risk real money.

Consensus Math

The trade is approved! But before buying, the Risk Manager calculates exactly how many coins to buy to ensure you never lose more than 2% of your account if the trade fails.

📱 Telegram & Database

The order is sent to the exchange. Simultaneously, it is permanently logged into your SQLite database for performance tracking, and a message is beamed to your phone.
